# Personal story editorial design

Warm paper (#f3f0e8), ink (#282a25), clay (#823d32) and muted sage (#dedfd2). Large Libre Caslon Display headings pair with DM Sans body copy. Real portrait and product photographs carry the story. Wide margins, rules and a restrained two-column layout create a personal editorial feel. On narrow screens the grids stack and navigation wraps. Links retain keyboard focus, pages use semantic headings, and reduced-motion preferences disable transitions.
